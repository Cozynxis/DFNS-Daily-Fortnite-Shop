ABOUT DFNS

MEET DFNS

DFNS — Daily Fortnite Shop is an unofficial fan-made project built to make the Fortnite Item Shop and cosmetic database simple, fast and enjoyable to explore.

What is DFNS?

DFNS gives you one clean place to check the current Item Shop, discover cosmetics and search through items that are not currently available in the shop.

Built for exploring

From outfits and emotes to pickaxes, gliders and more, DFNS is designed around finding cosmetics quickly and learning more about each item.

Fan-made project

DFNS is an independent community project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Epic Games.

Fresh shop data

The site uses publicly available Fortnite data to keep the shop and cosmetic information as useful and up to date as possible.

Your collection

With features such as Favorites and notifications, DFNS can help you keep track of cosmetics you care about and find them again later.

Made with care

DFNS is continuously improved with new features, UI tweaks and quality-of-life improvements to make browsing feel better.
